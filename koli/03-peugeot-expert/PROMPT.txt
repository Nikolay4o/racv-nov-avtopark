COLOUR DISCIPLINE
The navy blue must stay rich and readable against the dark set — deep and saturated, never washing out to grey or collapsing into black. The gold rim light along the roof edge and shoulder is what separates the van from the background.

STUDIO SETUP — identical for every image so the whole fleet matches
- Seamless dark studio cyclorama. Charcoal-to-black vertical gradient, about #151515 behind the vehicle fading to #050505 at the frame edges. No walls, no showroom, no furniture, no plants, no props.
- Floor: polished dark grey-black with a soft reflection at roughly 25% opacity, fading out within a metre. Not a mirror.
- Key light: one large soft overhead strip running the length of the van, drawing a clean highlight along the roof rail and shoulder.
- Rim light: warm gold, around 3200K, from the rear three-quarter, tracing the body edges. Clearly present, never blown out.
- Fill: subtle cool fill on the shadow side so no panel goes dead black.
- Camera: full-frame, 50mm equivalent, f/8, tripod at wheel-arch height. Because the vehicle is tall and long, step the camera back so it fits comfortably with margin. Keep verticals dead straight — no leaning walls, no wide-angle stretch.
- Finish: photorealistic editorial car photography. Glossy detailed paint, clean dark glass, matte black tyres. NOT a 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT an illustration.
- Output: landscape 3:2, maximum resolution, breathing room on all sides — the image will be cropped to 16:10 later.
