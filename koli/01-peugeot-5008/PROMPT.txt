COLOUR DISCIPLINE
Black on a dark set is the hardest shot in the fleet. Push the warm rim light so the roofline, the shoulder crease, the wheel arches and the front bumper edges are clearly drawn in warm light. Every panel keeps readable detail — the car must never collapse into a silhouette or a black blob.

STUDIO SETUP — identical for every image so the whole fleet matches
- Seamless dark studio cyclorama. Charcoal-to-black vertical gradient, about #151515 behind the car fading to #050505 at the frame edges. No walls, no showroom, no furniture, no plants, no windows, no props.
- Floor: polished dark grey-black with a soft, believable reflection of the car at roughly 25% opacity that fades out within a metre. Not a mirror.
- Key light: one large soft overhead strip running the length of the car, drawing a single clean highlight down the roofline and shoulder.
- Rim light: warm gold, around 3200K, from the rear three-quarter, tracing the edges of the bodywork. This is the signature of the set — clearly present, never blown out.
- Fill: subtle cool fill on the shadow side so no panel goes dead black.
- A faint warm halo behind the car separates it from the background. Nothing else in frame.
- Camera: full-frame, 50mm equivalent, f/8, tripod at wheel-arch height. Natural perspective, straight verticals, no wide-angle stretch.
- Finish: photorealistic editorial car photography. Freshly detailed glossy paint, clean dark glass, matte black tyres with no shine-spray look. Sharp throughout, rich blacks that still hold detail. NOT a 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT an illustration.
- Output: landscape 3:2, maximum resolution, breathing room on all sides — the image will be cropped to 16:10 later, so nothing important may touch the frame edge.
