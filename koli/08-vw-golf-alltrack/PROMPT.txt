Keep the dark plastic cladding clearly distinct from the body paint, and make the raised stance obvious — this car sits higher than a normal estate.

STUDIO SETUP — identical for every image so the whole fleet matches
- Seamless dark studio cyclorama. Charcoal-to-black vertical gradient, about #151515 behind the car fading to #050505 at the frame edges. No walls, no showroom, no furniture, no plants, no props.
- Floor: polished dark grey-black with a soft reflection at roughly 25% opacity, fading out within a metre. Not a mirror.
- Key light: one large soft overhead strip running the length of the car, drawing a clean highlight down the roofline and shoulder.
- Rim light: warm gold, around 3200K, from the rear three-quarter, tracing the body edges. Clearly present, never blown out.
- Fill: subtle cool fill on the shadow side so no panel goes dead black.
- Camera: full-frame, 50mm equivalent, f/8, tripod at wheel-arch height. Long estate — step back so the whole body fits with margin on both sides. Straight verticals, no wide-angle stretch.
- Finish: photorealistic editorial car photography. Glossy detailed paint, clean dark glass, matte black tyres. NOT a 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT an illustration.
- Output: landscape 3:2, maximum resolution, breathing room on all sides — the image will be cropped to 16:10 later.
