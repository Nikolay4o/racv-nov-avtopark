Промптове за новите снимки на колите

11 промпта, по един на кола. Всеки описва колата толкова подробно, че работи без прикачени снимки — само копираш и пействаш. Картинката отляво ти казва коя кола е кой промпт.

Как се ползва: нов чат в ChatGPT → пействаш промпта → излизат 3 кадъра един след друг (при вана 4). Ако спре след първия, пиши продължи с кадър 2. Една кола = един чат — иначе моделът почва да прелива цветове между колите.
Ако все пак можеш да прикачваш: отвори линковете „текущи снимки" под всяка кола, свали ги и ги прикачи към промпта. Тогава добави и този ред най-отгоре: Match the attached photos exactly for colour, wheels and trim — but ignore everything in them that is not the car (wall sign, QR code, phone number, icon strip, plate lettering). С прикачени снимки резултатът е още по-точен, без тях промптът пак си работи.

THE CAR
2018 Volkswagen Golf Alltrack, Mk7 estate with 4MOTION all-wheel drive. Champagne silver-beige metallic paint with a fine metallic flake. Silver multi-spoke alloy wheels. Raised ride height with dark grey plastic wheel-arch extensions and matching sill cladding running the length of the car. Brushed silver skid-plate elements under the front and rear bumpers, silver roof rails, chrome window surrounds, honeycomb lower grille, chrome grille bars with the VW roundel, "4MOTION" badge on the tailgate, twin chrome exhaust tips on the left side. Long estate roofline. Interior: black, steering wheel with the VW roundel and an ALLTRACK badge, twin analogue dials with red needles and a small display between them, a square central touchscreen in the dashboard, physical climate dials below, a DSG automatic gear selector on the console, dark brushed trim strips across the dashboard and doors.

COLOUR DISCIPLINE
The paint is a warm champagne silver, not plain grey. Under the gold rim light it should glow softly and show fine metallic sparkle in the highlights. Keep the dark plastic cladding clearly distinct from the body paint, and make the raised stance obvious — this car sits higher than a normal estate.

STUDIO SETUP — identical for every image so the whole fleet matches
- Seamless dark studio cyclorama. Charcoal-to-black vertical gradient, about #151515 behind the car fading to #050505 at the frame edges. No walls, no showroom, no furniture, no plants, no props.
- Floor: polished dark grey-black with a soft reflection at roughly 25% opacity, fading out within a metre. Not a mirror.
- Key light: one large soft overhead strip running the length of the car, drawing a clean highlight down the roofline and shoulder.
- Rim light: warm gold, around 3200K, from the rear three-quarter, tracing the body edges. Clearly present, never blown out.
- Fill: subtle cool fill on the shadow side so no panel goes dead black.
- Camera: full-frame, 50mm equivalent, f/8, tripod at wheel-arch height. Long estate — step back so the whole body fits with margin on both sides. Straight verticals, no wide-angle stretch.
- Finish: photorealistic editorial car photography. Glossy detailed paint, clean dark glass, matte black tyres. NOT a 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT an illustration.
- Output: landscape 3:2, maximum resolution, breathing room on all sides — the image will be cropped to 16:10 later.
